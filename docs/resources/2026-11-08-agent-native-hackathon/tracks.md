# Tracks and recognition categories

## There are NO tracks and NO sponsor bounties

- FAQ, "Do I have to pick a prescribed track?", verbatim: "No. Choose your own idea and intended users. Best
  Overall App, Best Productivity App, Most Creative App, and Best Use of Agent-Native recognize different
  strengths, not prescribed tracks. These 4 recognition categories carry no extra prize money; cash awards are
  ranked first through fifth."
- The only sponsor is Builder.io (Rules: "Sponsor: Builder.io."). No third-party sponsors or bounties are
  listed anywhere on the event site.

## The four recognition categories (non-cash)

Source: event page accordion "Recognition categories" (https://www.builder.io/hackathon, expanded in Chrome
2026-11-08). Descriptions are verbatim.

| Category | Description (verbatim) | Cash | Minimum bar / required tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best Overall App** | "The strongest combination of usefulness, technical execution, and user experience." | none | A working Agent-Native app plus a public Clips demo (the same entry requirements as every entry) |
| **Best Productivity App** | "The most effective application for helping people get things done, whether at work or in everyday life." | none | same |
| **Most Creative App** | "The most original or imaginative application of Agent-Native." | none | same |
| **Best Use of Agent-Native** | "Thoughtful, demonstrated use of the framework to connect agent capabilities, the interface, and application behavior. Naming or importing the framework alone is not enough." | none | same, plus *demonstrated* integration of agent, UI, and app behavior |

Page footnote: "Recognition categories do not represent additional cash awards."

## How they are awarded

- Rules, verbatim: "Best Overall App, Best Productivity App, Most Creative App, and Best Use of Agent-Native
  are recognition categories only. They do not create additional cash prizes or change the weighted scoring
  rubric."
- **No separate selection procedure is published** for the categories (no category-specific judges or
  rubric): **gap**. The only published scoring is the single weighted rubric (`event.md`).
- FAQ: "A project can receive at most one of the five ranked cash awards (Grand Prize or second through fifth
  place). Best Overall App, Best Productivity App, Most Creative App, and Best Use of Agent-Native are
  recognition categories with no extra prize money. Any approved mentorship and credits apply once per unique
  winning project, not once per award."

## What this means for idea-forge (facts only, no idea selection)

- All cash comes from **one ranking** on the 40/20/20/20 rubric. The categories line up with rubric axes:
  Productivity ↔ Usefulness and Execution, Creative ↔ Creativity and Design, Best Use ↔ Cleverness and Use of
  Agent-Native.
- The "Best Use" text and the rubric's 20% "Cleverness and Use of Agent-Native" both require the agent, the UI,
  and app behavior to be *connected through the framework* (shared actions, data, and state). See
  `sponsors/agent-native.md`.
